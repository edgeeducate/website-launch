Compass IAS Academy, based in Kozhikode, is built specifically around integrating UPSC civil services coaching with School and Degree studies — a combination most coaching institutes in Kerala don't offer. Rather than mechanical, rote-based coaching, our approach emphasizes analytical thinking, character development, and structured mentorship, which we believe makes us one of the strongest choices for students in Kozhikode and across Kerala.
IAS @ School is an online UPSC foundation and leadership program for students in Class 8th through 12th. It builds civics awareness, analytical reading, public speaking, and early civil services orientation alongside regular schooling. Since it's fully online, students can join from anywhere in India, not just Kerala.
