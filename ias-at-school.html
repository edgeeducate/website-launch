Why This Program

Why IAS @ School?

Most school tuition centers around scoring marks through memorization. IAS @ School takes a different approach: it treats thinking skills — critical reasoning, structured argument, current affairs analysis, and public speaking — as a life skill in their own right, not just UPSC preparation. The result is a student who scores better in school civics, history, and English, communicates with more confidence, and enters higher education (and eventually, competitive exams) with a genuine head start.

Who It's For

Any Class 8th–12th student, from any school board (State, CBSE, ICSE), anywhere in India — no prior UPSC knowledge needed.

How It's Delivered

Fully online via live interactive sessions — no travel needed, works alongside any school schedule across India.

⏰

Time Commitment

Structured weekend and evening sessions, deliberately scheduled around school hours, homework, and board exam periods.

What They Gain

Stronger civics/history/English performance now, and a multi-year head start on UPSC, Kerala PSC, or any competitive exam later.
